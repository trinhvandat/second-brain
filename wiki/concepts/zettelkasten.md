# Zettelkasten

A note-taking method by sociologist Niklas Luhmann, credited with helping him publish
50 books and 600+ articles by "working in partnership with his Zettelkasten"
(as of 2026-06, zettelkasten.de). (confidence: high)

Core principles:
- **Atomicity** — one knowledge building block per note, each with a unique address
  so it can be referenced and linked.
- **Contextual linking** — knowledge emerges only from links that explain *why* they
  connect; a bare link with no explanation creates no knowledge.

This vault inherits both principles: atomicity drives the `wiki/concepts` one-file-one-idea
rule, and the contextual-link rule is why every note must carry a contextual wikilink —
see [[llm-wiki]] (the LLM-over-Markdown pattern this vault is built on) and [[index]].

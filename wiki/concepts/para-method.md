# PARA Method

PARA is Tiago Forte's organizing framework: **P**rojects, **A**reas, **R**esources,
**A**rchives (as of 2026-06, toolfinder.com). (confidence: high)

- **Projects** — short-term efforts with a goal and deadline.
- **Areas** — long-term responsibilities to maintain (e.g. health, a team).
- **Resources** — topics of ongoing interest, reference material.
- **Archives** — inactive items from the other three.

It is the organizing scheme of [[basb]]. Forte introduced PARA in a 2017 blog post,
before the 2022 book. (confidence: high)

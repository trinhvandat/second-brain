# Personal Context Management (PCM)

Tiago Forte's reframing of personal knowledge management for the AI era: the bottleneck
of AI in knowledge work is missing *personal context*, not model capability — "better
results come from better context", and "your AI doesn't really know you. That's the
problem" (as of 2026-06, buildingasecondbrain.com/ai-second-brain). (confidence: high)

PCM is the skill of structuring notes, files, and systems so an LLM can access them
effectively. It is the AI-era evolution of [[basb]], and it is exactly the motivation
behind this vault's [[llm-wiki]] design — keep knowledge in Markdown an LLM can read.
A common tool for doing this hands-on is [[obsidian]].

# Leonard's Second Brain

A personal knowledge base of compiled, atomic notes — connected with wikilinks.
Use the **explorer** on the left to browse, or **search** (press `Ctrl/⌘ + K`) to jump anywhere.

## Sections

- **[[augmented-llm|Concepts]]** — foundational ideas, methods, and patterns
  (agentic systems, BASB, Zettelkasten, PARA, and more).
- **[[obsidian|Entities]]** — tools, people, companies, and projects.
- **[[claude-models|Tech]]** — technical reference notes.

## How to read this

Notes are **atomic** (one idea each) and linked with `[[wikilinks]]`. Follow the links to
explore related ideas, and check the **graph** and **backlinks** panels to see how a note
connects to the rest of the brain.

> [!note] Live knowledge base
> Notes carry a `status` (current / superseded / disputed) and an `updated` date.
> This site is generated from the vault and redeploys automatically on every update.
